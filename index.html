Artisanal Standards

Why Our Bakes Taste Remarkably Different

We built Page 26 with a singular obsession: zero compromises on chocolate, dairy, or time.

Pure Couverture Chocolate

Commercial bakeries use compound chocolate loaded with vegetable palm fats. We exclusively use pure couverture chocolate with real cocoa butter that melts luxuriously at body temperature.

100% Exclusively Eggless

No compromises on texture. Through careful emulsion of cultured cream, whole dairy, and pure extracts, our cheesecakes achieve the authentic dense crumb of traditional Philadelphia bakeries.

Slow-Baked to Order (7 Days)

Nothing sits in a freezer or on a display shelf. When you place a pre-order, ingredients are procured fresh and your bake is slow-baked in small batches just before dispatch.

Artisanal Real Fruit Compotes

Our blueberries and strawberries are simmered slowly in-house into luscious compotes, providing natural brightness that pairs perfectly with rich baked cream cheese.
